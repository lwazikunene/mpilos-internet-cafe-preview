Our story

From a bedroom desk to our own doors.

Mpilo's Internet Cafe was founded in 2021 by Mpilo Biyela, who was 25 as of September 2026. He struggled to find a permanent job after matric. He used savings from a six-month internship to buy a second-hand laptop and printer, and began offering printing services to friends and neighbours in Shayamoya.

As business improved, he moved from his bedroom to the veranda, and then to dedicated premises in Shakaskraal.

  1. 2021

    The first printer

    A second-hand laptop and printer, and printing for friends and neighbours in Shayamoya.

  2. The veranda

    Out of the bedroom

    As business improved, the work moved from his bedroom to the veranda.

  3. 2023

    Dedicated premises

    The cafe moved into its own premises in Shakaskraal.

“It's all about taking the first step. If you fail, it's not the end; learn, adjust and start again.”Mpilo Biyela, founder
